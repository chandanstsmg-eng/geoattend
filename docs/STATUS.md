## Known limitations and assumptions

* Single organisation (no multi-tenancy). One current workplace per employee; several shifts allowed.
* `CURRENT_DATE` for assignment effective dates is the database's (UTC) date. Shift windows use the shift's own time zone.
* Rate limits are in-memory per API instance. Use a shared store or proxy limits when scaling out.
* iOS: architecture and config are present but untested; iOS may defer background updates for stationary devices.
* The default distance filter is 0 m (time-driven updates), so stationary phones keep reporting; measure the battery impact on devices.
* GPS spoofing cannot be fully prevented; mock-location flags are risk indicators only. Photos are not identity verification.
* `npm audit` in `apps/mobile` reports findings in `@expo/cli` dev tooling only (not shipped in the app).

## Owner's decisions on Phase C (4 Nov 2026)

Regression tests: `corrections-and-photos.test.ts`. External requirements: [EXTERNAL-DEPENDENCIES.md](EXTERNAL-DEPENDENCIES.md).

- Voided records stay as history and count in no attendance, hours, lateness, overtime or pay figure. Overtime on a voided shift stays recorded but leaves every total and the approval queue, and cannot be approved.

## Mobile reliability (4 Nov 2026)

On branch `phase-d-mobile`. Audit: [MOBILE-AUDIT.md](MOBILE-AUDIT.md). Phone tests: [ANDROID-DEVICE-TEST-PLAN.md](ANDROID-DEVICE-TEST-PLAN.md). What is still unverified: [PENDING-VERIFICATION.md](PENDING-VERIFICATION.md).

- Saved locations are removed only on the server's answer. Ending a shift, losing the sign-in, a 403 or starting the next shift no longer discards them.
- Check-ins and shift ends can be saved without a connection and are sent later. The server judges them on their evidence, flags them `OFFLINE_CAPTURE` or `OFFLINE_END`, and never accepts one earlier than the session start it recorded. Companies set the limit (`offlineAttendanceHours`, default 24; 0 turns it off).
- A record sent late is `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, never `VERIFIED`. It is in history with its label but counts towards nothing (attendance, hours, overtime, payroll) until a supervisor or owner approves it (`MANUAL_APPROVED`, with their name) or rejects it with a reason (an owner can override). Its hours are shown apart as pending. The device's own time stays in `evidence_client_time`, and the time the server received it in `device_meta.receivedAt`.
- Moving an employee to another shift takes effect from the assignment's date, and past attendance keeps its shift and figures. Editing a shift's own times takes effect from the day of the edit; to change times from a future date, assign people to a new shift from that date.
- Starting a shift, breaks and overtime still need a connection.
- A supervisor's face rejection applies at once; an owner can override it (migration 023).
- **Not tested on a real phone.** Offline behaviour must not be claimed to customers until the device plan passes.

## Dates and company isolation (5 Nov 2026)

- Business dates follow [TIME-AND-DATES.md](TIME-AND-DATES.md). Every use of the database's own date and of the server's zone is gone, on the API, the dashboard and the phone. Before this, "today" stayed on yesterday until 05:30 IST in about twenty places.
- Row-level security now backs up the application's company filters: [COMPANY-ISOLATION.md](COMPANY-ISOLATION.md). Requests and background jobs connect with a separate restricted login. Production refuses to start with an unsafe login.
- Background jobs run one company at a time, and one failure no longer stops the rest. Leases, back-off, failure records and crash recovery: [BACKGROUND-JOBS.md](BACKGROUND-JOBS.md).
- Load tested with 10 companies, 10,000 employees and 810,000 attendance records. Missing indexes had made the main reports time out; they do not now. The attendance list is paged before it is computed, long CSVs are streamed, and `/employees` honours a limit.
- Each test run has its own throw-away database server and connects as a non-superuser, like production.
- Records waiting for verification no longer count. `attendance_work` leaves them out, so hours, overtime and everything built on them are official figures; `attendance_work_all` keeps them for the separate pending hours. `hours.csv` and `overtime.csv` keep their agreed columns and official figures only; pending hours are on the hours and monthly reports, in `attendance.csv`, `monthly.csv` and `shift.csv`.
- Physical Android testing stays NOT VERIFIED. The APK from Phase D must not be distributed: its server address is a placeholder.
